**Full description:**

KÜLHAT: Last Delivery is a three-lane runner and shooter. Cross a Western town and a mountain trail through 40 stages, up to 120 stars, then keep running in endless mode after the campaign.

How to play
- Swipe left or right to change lanes.
- Swipe up to jump and down to slide under a low beam.
- Tap a wagon, barrel, or target to shoot. Hold to keep firing.
- At a fork, stay left, center, or right. The mine and the cave run longer. Rooftops and the ridge are shorter and riskier.

What's inside
- Two maps: Western Town and Mountain Trail.
- Barrels, mine carts, horse wagons, and a pursuer on your heels.
- Gold, gems, an armoury, and costumes that change your look only.
- A daily reward and three daily tasks, stored on the device. No server.
- Turkish and English.

This version has no ads and no in-app purchases. There is no account. Progress stays on the device. Internet is not required.
