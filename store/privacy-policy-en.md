# Privacy policy — KÜLHAT: Last Delivery

Last updated: 29 October 2026

KÜLHAT: Last Delivery is an offline game. This Google Play build has no ads, analytics, accounts, or payments.

## Data we collect

No personal data is sent to the developer. There is no server, account, advertising ID, or analytics SDK.

## Stored on the device

Progress (gold, gems, unlocked stages, stars, costume, audio and graphics choices, language) stays in the app's local storage. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Network

Internet is not required to play. The app does not transmit gameplay data.

## Children

The game is a simple action game suitable for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Contact

Use the developer email shown on the Play Console listing for privacy questions. This draft is for the store kit. Nothing has been published.
